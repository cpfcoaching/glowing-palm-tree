## 🎧 Hospitality to Cyber Operations & Leadership | Anthony Merlas | Breaking Into Cybersecurity

**Guest:** Anthony Merlas (Cybersecurity Operations & Threat Management Leader)

### Episode Summary:
How do you transition from managing high-pressure hospitality operations into leading IT security and risk operations? In this episode, Anthony Merlas shares his journey breaking into cybersecurity and how crisis de-escalation, customer empathy, and cross-functional communication became his superpowers in security operations.

### ⏱️ Episode Chapters & Timestamps:
- **00:00** - Introduction & Anthony Merlas Background
- **03:15** - From Hospitality Management to Information Security
- **09:40** - Translating Crisis De-escalation into Incident Response
- **16:20** - Overcoming Imposter Syndrome in Technical Interviews
- **24:10** - Building a Hands-on Lab vs Cold Applying
- **32:45** - Advice for Mid-Career Pivoters & Rising Leaders

### 💡 Key Takeaways:

- Networking through community events and podcast participation accelerates interview callbacks.

**Guest:** Mike Lossman (Security Leadership Strategist & CISO Advisor)

Transitioning from a hands-on technical engineer into a strategic security leader requires an entirely different playbook. Mike Lossman joins Christophe Foulon to unpack the leadership principles, executive presence, and business risk alignment needed to reach the CISO level.

### ⏱️ Episode Chapters & Timestamps:

- **21:00** - Managing High-Performing Security Teams
