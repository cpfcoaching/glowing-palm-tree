**Guest:** Jim West — *Cybersecurity Leader, CISSP-ISSEP, Award-Winning Author & Speaker*

### Episode Summary:
Jim West broke into cybersecurity without a traditional tech background and without spending thousands on expensive training. He walked into a panel interview, told the truth about what he did not know, and got the job. In this episode, he shares the exact playbook he used: start with the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity free certification, build your LinkedIn presence from day one, and use authenticity in interviews as a differentiator rather than a liability. His message is direct: the entry path is more accessible than the industry wants you to believe.

### 💡 Key Takeaways:
- Start with free training: ISC2 CC is the right first step:** The ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity certification is free to pursue and designed specifically for career changers. Jim argues it gives you a credible signal to employers without requiring a financial bet before your first paycheck. It teaches fundamentals and gets your resume past the first filter.
- LinkedIn is not optional and waiting is a mistake:** Jim built his LinkedIn profile before he had a single cybersecurity credential and used it as a daily networking tool. The strategy is immediate: connect with practitioners, comment thoughtfully on posts, and let visibility compound. Most entry-level candidates wait until they feel ready. Jim says that is backwards.
- Honesty in panel interviews is a competitive advantage:** Interviewers can tell when candidates bluff their way through technical questions. Jim's approach was to answer directly when he knew something and say clearly when he did not. That transparency built trust with the hiring panel and was cited as a deciding factor in his offer. Authenticity is a skill, and it is one most candidates are not practicing.

## 🎧 How I Built an Email Security Company and Made My First Million | Tal Kollender

**Guest:** Tal Kollender — *CEO, Remedio*

### Episode Summary:
Tal Kollender built an email security company from the ground up and turned it into a million-dollar business. In this episode, he shares the unfiltered story behind that journey: from identifying a gap in SMB security to closing his first enterprise clients, to solving a problem most people overlook: print and endpoint vulnerabilities that leave SMBs exposed without ever realizing it. He also challenges the credentials-first mindset that dominates hiring in cybersecurity and makes a clear case for why startups reward execution over diplomas.

### 💡 Key Takeaways:
- Startups value results over credentials:** Tal is direct: most early-stage security companies don't care whether you have a degree or a CISSP. They want to see what you have shipped, sold, or fixed. Certs are a signal; output is the proof.
- SMB and print vulnerabilities are massively underserved:** The attack surface created by printers, shared drives, and unmanaged endpoints in small businesses is enormous and largely ignored by the market. Tal built a product around single-click remediation specifically because SMBs lack the IT staff to handle complex tooling.
- The first million is a mindset shift:** Revenue did not come from a perfect product. It came from ruthless focus on one painful problem, a willingness to stay close to customers, and the discipline to say no to feature requests that would dilute the core.
