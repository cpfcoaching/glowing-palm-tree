2. Root Cause: Why None of the Progress Appeared to Persist

The disconnect between what appeared on screen during Autofix versus what remained after reloading stemmed from three interrelated factors:

  • The YouTube Brand Account Permission Trap (HTTP 403 Forbidden): Your channel Breaking Into Cybersecurity / CPF Coaching is a YouTube Brand Account. When signing in with Google OAuth (christophefoulon@gmail.com), Google issued an OAuth token for your personal user identity rather than delegating it to the Brand Account channel. When the app called PUT https://www.googleapis.com/youtube/v3/videos, YouTube responded with 403 Forbidden: Channel Brand Account mismatch.
  • The In-Memory Simulation Loop (The "Demo Feel"): When YouTube rejected the live write with 403, the app cleared the expired token, but did not stop the loop. Instead, it simulated delays (setTimeout 800ms), claimed 0 defects were resolved, and set score = 98 and CTA ACTIVE: YES in React local state. To your eyes, each card turned green—but YouTube's servers never accepted the changes.
  • The Sync Reset to Reality: When you refreshed or synced videos from YouTube, the app pulled the live, unmodified YouTube video data. Because YouTube never received the write, the live descriptions still lacked the CTA link, dropping the live score back to 85/100 with 1 issue: Missing CTA link.
The Problem Identified: The app was pretending in-memory that an update succeeded even when YouTube's live API failed. This created the illusion of a demo mode.
