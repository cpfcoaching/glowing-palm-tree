🛠️ The Career Playbook: The 3-Project Rule

I spoke with several hiring managers this week, and their biggest complaint was unanimous: “We receive 600 applications for an entry-level SOC or analyst role, and 95% of resumes are identical keyword lists with zero demonstrable proof of work.”

The 3-Project Portfolio Framework:
  1. 🧪 Homelab Detection Engineering: Simulated alerts in Wazuh/Elastic with custom detection rules.
  2. 🛡️ Lightweight Threat Modeling: STRIDE breakdown of a cloud app architecture.
  3. 📄 Executive Incident Post-Mortem: 1-page non-technical executive summary for leadership.
