## 🎯 The 3-Project Rule: Stop Spraying 500 Resumes

I spoke with three hiring managers this week, and they all echoed the same frustration: *“We receive 600 applications for an entry-level SOC role, and 90% have identical keyword-stuffed resumes with zero evidence of hands-on troubleshooting.”*

If you want to cut through the noise, you don't need 10 more certifications. You need **three high-signal projects**:

1. **Detection Engineering in a Homelab:** Spin up an Ubuntu VM with Wazuh or Elastic Security. Trigger a simulated Sysmon alert using Atomic Red Team, document the log telemetry, and write a detection rule.
2. **Threat Modeling Breakdown:** Take an open-source SaaS architecture or e-commerce workflow and perform a lightweight STRIDE threat model. Document the trust boundaries and mitigations.
3. **Executive Incident Communication:** Write a 1-page post-mortem summarizing a simulated ransomware incident in language a CFO or CEO would immediately understand.

When you send a 2-minute Loom video or GitHub link walking through one of these projects directly to a hiring manager, your callback rate jumps from **1% to over 30%**.

## 🛡️ vCISO Perspective: Practical AI & Vendor Risk Governance

For business owners and security leaders: **Your biggest AI risk isn't an omnipotent rogue model—it's unmonitored employee data leakage into public LLMs and third-party vendor dependencies.**

Before buying another security tool, answer three questions:
* What data are your teams putting into generative AI tools today?
* Which critical vendors have access to your customer records or source code?
* Do you have an incident response playbook if a critical SaaS partner goes offline?
